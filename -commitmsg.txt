Replace the Gemini path with keyless live-source scraping

The record for a searched company or creator is now built from real
public sources, with no API key and no account:

- Wikidata resolves the query (ranked so taxa, regions, towns,
  disambiguation pages and other lookalikes lose to real organizations
  and people; humans resolve as creators) and yields the official
  website, YouTube/X handles and industry labels
- Wikipedia supplies the summary text and lead image
- The official site is read through the free r.jina.ai proxy (no-cache
  mode), with tracker redirects refused, reader preamble stripped, and
  each fetched page keeping its provenance (URL + excerpt)
- Transparency signals found in the scraped text (disclosures, sourcing,
  reports, corrections, conflict-of-interest mentions) become traceable
  evidence with page-level provenance
- The score becomes a deterministic evidence-coverage metric: how much
  first-party material was actually found publicly, not a verdict
- Gemini service, hardcoded key and ApiKeyInput are removed entirely;
  search falls back to the sample catalog, labeled as such, when public
  sources hold nothing
- Fix Wikidata item-link parsing (item claims arrive as {id} objects,
  which silenced the human/creator distinction and the class filters)
